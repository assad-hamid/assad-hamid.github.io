Metrobus Accessibility & Transport Equity Analysis

QGIS · OpenStreetMap · WorldPop

Integrated open transit data with 100 m population rasters, 500 m / 1 km service buffers, and 15-minute walking isochrones to evaluate equitable access to mass transit in Islamabad.

The analysis found that 28.1% of the metropolitan population was within 1 km of transit, while 82% of mapped low-income residents were underserved. These findings connect spatial analysis with transport equity and inclusive-access planning.

U.S. Crash Severity Analysis

Python · NHTSA CRSS, 2016–2023

Analyzed crash-, vehicle-, and person-level records to investigate injury severity in relation to intersection type, collision patterns, environmental conditions, and road-user characteristics.

The project identified regional and intersection-related safety patterns, greater severity in front-to-front collisions, and an increased injury burden in alcohol-related crashes. Recommendations addressed intersection upgrades, daytime enforcement, speed management, weather advisories, and ADAS incentives.

Proposed M-7 Motorway — Traffic Feasibility Study

Academic project · PTV VISUM · HDM-4 · HCM-CALC

  • Developed a 12-zone, multiclass PTV VISUM model for the Hub–Dadu corridor and calibrated origin–destination demand against classified traffic counts.
  • Compared with- and without-motorway scenarios using user-equilibrium assignment and travel-time skims to assess traffic diversion and accessibility improvements.
  • Estimated vehicle operating costs with HDM-4 and assessed cost-based demand responses; forecast traffic to 2053 and evaluated mainline capacity and level of service with HCM-CALC.
