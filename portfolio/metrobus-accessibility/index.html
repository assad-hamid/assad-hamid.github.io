Metrobus Accessibility & Transport Equity Analysis

QGIS · OpenStreetMap · WorldPop

Research question

How equitably does mass transit serve Islamabad’s population, particularly low-income communities?

My contribution

Integrated open transit and population data to map service coverage and identify underserved communities.

Methods

Combined OpenStreetMap transit data and 100 m WorldPop population rasters in QGIS, using 500 m and 1 km service buffers and 15-minute walking isochrones.

Key findings

The analysis estimated that 28.1% of the metropolitan population was within 1 km of transit and that 82% of mapped low-income residents were underserved. These results highlight gaps in spatial access to transit.
