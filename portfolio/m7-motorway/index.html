Proposed M-7 Motorway — Traffic Feasibility Study

Academic project · PTV VISUM · HDM-4 · HCM-CALC

Research question

How would the proposed M-7 motorway affect corridor traffic distribution, travel times, and future mainline operating conditions?

My contribution

Developed a 12-zone, multiclass VISUM model for the Hub–Dadu corridor and calibrated origin–destination demand against classified traffic counts.

Methods

Compared with- and without-motorway scenarios using user-equilibrium assignment and travel-time skims. Estimated operating costs with HDM-4, assessed cost-based demand responses, and forecast traffic to 2053 for HCM-CALC capacity and level-of-service analysis.

Key findings

The model estimated a Hub–Dadu travel-time reduction of approximately 2.27 hours. The assumed three-lane-per-direction mainline remained at LOS A through 2053 under the selected demand and operating assumptions. This academic study assesses traffic and operations; it does not establish financial feasibility.
